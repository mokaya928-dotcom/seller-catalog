# PART 1: Finding 50+ Leads Today

You don't need paid ads or cold calls. Kenya has hundreds of thousands of sellers whose entire livelihood depends on WhatsApp groups and status. Here is how to find 50+ active sellers in 30 minutes:

### 1. Facebook Marketplace & Seller Groups (Goldmine for WhatsApp Links)
Join these massive Facebook groups (search directly on Facebook):
- **Nairobi Kilimani/Westlands Business Hub**
- **Nairobi Thika Road Business Forum**
- **Eastleigh Business Community / Garissa Lodge Wholesalers**
- **Kenya Thrift Clothes & Shoes Wholesale/Retail**
- **Nairobi Organic Groceries & Farm Fresh Suppliers**

**What to search inside these groups:**
Type in the group search bar:
> `"chat.whatsapp.com"` or `"join my WhatsApp group"` or `"WhatsApp 07"`

**The Result:** You will find dozens of posts like:
*"Join my new arrivals shoe group here: chat.whatsapp.com/XXXXX"*
*"Weekly thrift bales and retail dresses: WhatsApp group link..."*
Click the link, join the group, or grab the admin’s phone number immediately.

---

### 2. Instagram Bio Hack
Open Instagram search, tap **Tags** or **Places**:
- Search hashtags: `#kenyanshop`, `#nairobiboutique`, `#nairobishoes`, `#mombasafashion`, `#kenyathrift`, `#nairobihair`, `#cakebakersnairobi`
- Search places: *Jamia Mall, Imenti House, Yare Towers Eastleigh, City Market Nairobi*

Look at their Instagram Bio:
- If their bio says: *"Order via WhatsApp: 07XX XXX XXX"* or *"Tap link to join our WhatsApp VIP catalog group"*, they are prime targets!
- **Do NOT pitch on Instagram comments.** Save their WhatsApp number or send them the human Instagram DM script.

---

### 3. TikTok Live & TikTok Shop Bio Search
Open TikTok search and search:
> `"Nairobi wholesale"`, `"Nairobi boutique"`, `"affordable shoes Nairobi"`, `"duvet covers Nairobi"`

Filter by **Videos posted this week** or go to **LIVE**.
- When small sellers go live showing their clothes/shoes, their pinned comment is ALWAYS: *"WhatsApp 07XXXXXXXX to order or join our group"*.
- Take that number and WhatsApp them directly.

---

### 4. Walking Nairobi Malls (Walk-in Lead Collection)
Walk through:
- **Jamia Mall** (Bags, perfumes, shoes)
- **Imenti House / Veteran House** (Streetwear, sneakers)
- **Perida Centre / Tom Mboya Stalls** (Electronics, phone accessories)
- **Eastleigh Bangkok / Olympic Plaza** (Wholesale and retail fabrics, abayas)

Every single stall has a poster on the wall with: **"WhatsApp Us: 07XXXXXXXX"**.
Take 15 minutes to photograph 30 stall posters on your phone. When you sit down for lunch, text them using **Script 1**.

# PART 2: The Live Demo Playbook

When a seller responds to your Hook with: *"Yes / Send it / Nitumie / Show me"*, here is how you deliver the link to blow them away:

### 1. The Demo URL
Use your deployed link (e.g. `https://your-seller-demo.vercel.app` or `https://your-catalog.netlify.app`).

### 2. The 3 Actions You Tell Them to Take
Do not just send a raw link without instructions. Tell them exactly what 3 buttons to press:

```text
Awesome, check it out here:
👉 [Insert your Demo Link]

Open it on your phone and try these 3 things:
1. Tap any product — notice the clean price and how pressing "Order via WhatsApp" types the exact order ready for you.
2. Tap the Flyer Icon on that product — look at how it instantly creates promotional poster flyers with different colors, styles, and captions for your Status in 1 click.
3. Notice that nothing expires — your items stay live permanently until you remove them.

Take a look and tell me what you think!
```

### 3. Why This Works:
- They experience the customer's journey in 5 seconds.
- They realize they never have to pay a graphic designer for WhatsApp Status posters again.
- They see that their phone gallery won't be clogged with 1,000 screenshots anymore.

# PART 3: The 15-Minute Fulfillment System
*(How to set up a paying client in 15 minutes after they send you KES 1,000)*

You must make onboarding effortless for them. **Never give them homework or tell them to fill out complicated spreadsheets.**

### The "Small But Smart" KES 1,000 Pricing:
- **Price:** KES 1,000 flat (no monthly fees).
- **Pitch:** *"Less than the profit of selling just ONE pair of shoes or dress."*
- **Speed:** Setup in 15 minutes.

### Step 1: Collect Their Info via WhatsApp (3 Minutes)
Send them this simple WhatsApp checklist:
```text
Karibu sana! Let's get your catalog live in 20 minutes.
Send me:
1. Your Official Shop / Brand Name
2. Your WhatsApp phone number for receiving orders
3. Your Lipa na M-Pesa Buy Goods Till or Paybill number (optional)
4. Your logo or shop profile picture
5. Forward me 15–20 photos of your top-selling products directly here on WhatsApp with their prices.
```

### Step 2: Upload Products into the Catalog (7 Minutes)
1. Open the admin/seller dashboard or `starterData.js` / Supabase backend.
2. Upload the photos they forwarded you.
3. Type the product title and price (e.g., *"Classic Leather Loafers - KES 3,500"*).
4. Select the category (Shoes, Fashion, Beauty, etc.).
5. Tap Save.

### Step 3: Configure WhatsApp Number & M-Pesa (2 Minutes)
1. In Settings, set:
   - `Seller Name`: e.g. "Glamour Footwear Nairobi"
   - `WhatsApp Order Number`: e.g. `254712345678`
   - `Currency`: `KES`
   - `Till / Paybill`: e.g. `Buy Goods 894321`

### Step 4: Handover & Training (1 Minute)
Send them their official store link and their Group Announcement template:

```text
🎉 Your official product catalog is LIVE!

👉 Your Catalog Link: [Insert Their Unique Link]

Here is a ready-made message you can copy and post into your WhatsApp Group right now:
------------------------------------------
"Good morning everyone! 🌟
To stop flooding your phones with 50 photos every day, we now have our official permanent catalog! 

You can browse all our available items, sizes, and prices anytime 24/7 here:
👉 [Insert Their Unique Link]

Simply tap any item to order directly to our WhatsApp. Enjoy browsing!"
------------------------------------------

Pin that link in your WhatsApp Group Description so new members always see your stock!
```

| **11:30 AM - 2:00 PM** | Reply to positive responses with Demo Link (Step 2) | 12 - 15 demos opened |

### The Volume Math:
- 30 Messages sent per day
- ~12 to 15 replies asking to see the preview
- ~7 to 8 quick conversations
- **5 to 8 closed sales per day @ KES 1,000 each = KES 5,000 to KES 8,000 daily cash (KES 150,000+ / month).**
- Plus backend upsells: KES 300 to upload new stock arrivals, KES 500 for custom weekend flash-sale poster packs.
