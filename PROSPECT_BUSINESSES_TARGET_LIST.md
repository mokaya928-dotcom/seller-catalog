## 🎯 Executive Summary: Why These Businesses Will Pay

Like **Orewa Limited** (`orewa.co.ke`), these 20 merchants share the same operational DNA:
1. **High SKU Count (50–500+ items)**: Phones, sneakers, luxury bags, K-beauty, designer perfumes, and home appliances.
2. **Posting Fatigue**: They post 10 to 25 items on WhatsApp Status and Instagram Stories every morning. Making posters by hand takes 1–2 hours daily or costs KES 500+ per custom flyer.
3. **DM Friction**: 80% of customer inquiries are repetitive (*"How much?", "Is size 42 available?", "Send more angles", "Till number?"*).
