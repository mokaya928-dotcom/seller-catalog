## Why WhatsApp Group & Status Sellers Are Your #1 Easiest Customers
These sellers deal with two massive headaches every single day:
1. **WhatsApp Group Storage Spam:** When they dump 30–60 product photos in their group daily, members mute the group, complain about full phone memory, or exit completely.
2. **WhatsApp Status Disappearing:** WhatsApp Status vanishes after 24 hours. If a customer joins today or wants to see last week's stock, it's gone. The seller wastes hours answering: *"Uko na hii bado? How much is this?"* while scrolling through 1,000 gallery photos.

---

## The 4 Golden Rules Before You Text Them
1. **Never text like an AI bot:** No formal "Dear Sir/Madam", no robotic intros, no bullet-point essays.
2. **First statement must be crystal clear:** *"This is NOT a website."* It’s a **permanent digital catalog shop** for their stock.
3. **Products never leave unless deleted:** Unlike Status or groups where posts get lost, products stay there permanently.
4. **Re-Marketing Superpower:** They can repeatedly repost those same products to their Status using **different poster templates, fresh captions, and colors** in 1 tap without shooting new photos.
5. **NEVER drop the link in Message 1:** Always ask permission first. Only send the link and preview after they say *"Yes / Sure / Nitumie"*.
