### 🎬 SCENE 1: The 1-Tap Install / Clip to Phone Home Screen (00:00 – 00:15) ⭐ *THE BIG HOOK*
* **Screen Action:**
  - Show the mobile browser opening your demo link.
  - The gold/emerald prompt pops up at the bottom: **"Install App (1-Tap)"** (or on iOS: **"Add to Home Screen"**).
  - Tap the button. Show the quick confirmation.
  - Cut/Show your phone's actual home screen: the branded app icon appears right on your screen next to WhatsApp!
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Bro, here is the biggest game changer: You don't need to go to Google Play or App Store to download a heavy app, and you NEVER have to open a browser and search bookmarks every morning.*  
  > *You open the link ONCE, tap **'Install App'**, and boom — it clips right onto your phone home screen like a native app. It's right there next to WhatsApp every single day."*

### 🎬 SCENE 2: Launch App & 1-Click First Post Download (00:15 – 00:28)
* **Screen Action:**
  - From the phone home screen, tap the newly installed app icon.
  - The app launches instantly in standalone full-screen mode (zero browser address bars, zero tabs, pure native app look).
  - Directly in the **"Today"** view, the morning post is already rendered (e.g. *L'Oreal Absolut Repair Oil* or *CeraVe Cleanser*).
  - Tap the green **"Download Flyer"** (or Share to WhatsApp) button.
  - The 9:16 studio flyer renders in 0.5s and saves straight to the phone gallery.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every morning, you just tap the icon from your home screen. No loading Canva, no searching for photos.*  
  > *Your morning deal is already waiting for you. Tap **'Download'** — and in 2 seconds, a high-converting 9:16 WhatsApp Status flyer with your official price and contacts is saved to your phone gallery. Done in 2 seconds. Zero graphic designer needed."*
* 🔥 **Why This Blows Their Mind:** They go from "wake up" to "ready-to-post status flyer" in 2 taps.

### 🎬 SCENE 3: Auto-Generated Captions (00:28 – 00:40)
* **Screen Action:**
  - Scroll down slightly under the flyer to the Caption box.
  - Show the pre-written caption with product benefits, official price, M-Pesa Till number, and ordering link.
  - Tap the language selector: switch between **English** and **Kenyan Mix / Sheng**.
  - Tap the blue **"Copy Caption"** button. The green checkmark *"Copied!"* confirms it.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"And you don't even have to write a caption. The system writes the sales pitch for you with product benefits, your M-Pesa Buy Goods Till, and ordering instructions.*  
  > *Toggle between English or Kenyan selling slang, tap **'Copy Caption'**, and paste it straight onto your WhatsApp Status."*
* 🔥 **Why This Blows Their Mind:** Eliminates repetitive typing of prices, Till numbers, and delivery terms.

### 🎬 SCENE 4: Flexible Schedule (5, 10, or 20 Posts & Zero Repeats) (00:40 – 00:52)
* **Screen Action:**
  - Tap the batch limit pills: tap **"10 Posts"** or **"20 Posts"**.
  - Show the queue instantly updating to 10 distinct products spread throughout the day (Morning Commute, Lunch Break, Afternoon Pick-Me-Up, Evening Rush, Bedtime Orders).
  - Show that every single poster is a **different unique product** — zero duplicate posters during the same day.
  - Tap the checkmark **"Mark as Posted"**. The card turns green with a completion check.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Want to post 5 items in the morning, 10 items, or 20 items a day? Just tap the batch size. The system schedules them across your day with zero duplicates—every poster is a unique product.*  
  > *Tomorrow, it automatically rotates to the next batch. Tap **'Mark as Posted'** so your sales team never double-posts or misses a rush hour."*
* 🔥 **Why This Blows Their Mind:** Full flexibility. They aren't locked into a rigid 5 posts. They can flood WhatsApp status with 10–20 unique deals without repeating products.

* **Screen Action:**
  - Tap the top ratio switch from **"Status (9:16)"** to **"Group / Feed (4:5)"**.
  - The flyer automatically re-shapes into portrait feed format without cropping the product.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Need to post in a WhatsApp Group or Instagram Feed instead of Status?*  
  > *Tap the ratio switch — the whole graphic instantly adapts to 4:5 feed size with zero distortion and zero cropping."*
* 🔥 **Why This Blows Their Mind:** Works for WhatsApp Status, WhatsApp Groups, and Instagram Feed simultaneously.

  - Tap **"Save Poster"**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"What if a customer DMs you on WhatsApp asking about a specific item?*  
  > *Jump into your **Products tab**, search your entire inventory in 1 second, and generate a branded flyer on the spot to send them in chat."*
* 🔥 **Why This Blows Their Mind:** Instant visual response closes DM customers on the spot.
