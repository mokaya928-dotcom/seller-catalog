### The Golden Rule of This Pitch:
1. **First statement must be crystal clear:** *"This is NOT a website."* It is a **permanent catalog shop** for your products.
2. **The Big Problem it solves:** WhatsApp Status and IG stories disappear after 24 hours or get buried under 100 new posts. Here, your products **never get lost or deleted unless you delete them yourself**.
3. **The Killer Re-Marketing Feature:** You can repeatedly repost and market those same products to your WhatsApp status using **different templates, captions, and colors** in one tap without taking new photos.
4. **Never send the link in message 1:** Only share the link and preview AFTER they respond with interest.
